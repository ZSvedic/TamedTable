TamedTable demo voiceover — for ElevenLabs (no SSML; uses its <break> tags).

ElevenLabs ignores SSML but honors <break time="x.xs" /> for pauses and reads
punctuation for cadence. Two ways to use this:

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION A — one paste (simplest). Paste the block below and generate one take.
The <break> values are sized so each line lands near its beat; pace varies by
voice, so treat them as approximate. Export the WAV and send it back — the video
gets retimed to your audio, so it doesn't have to be exact.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Talk to your data. <break time="0.7s" /> Real data is messy. Formats vary by country. <break time="1.4s" /> Just say what you want. <break time="1.1s" /> Watch every row change, right in front of you. <break time="1.8s" /> Ask in any language. <break time="1.2s" /> Keep the steps, or export to Python.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION B — per line (best sync). Generate each line as its own clip; I place
them at these exact start times. Cleanest result, a little more clicking.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

0.0s   Talk to your data.
2.0s   Real data is messy. Formats vary by country.
6.5s   Just say what you want.
9.0s   Watch every row change, right in front of you.
13.5s  Ask in any language.
16.0s  Keep the steps, or export to Python.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SETTINGS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Voice:  a warm, friendly narration voice (browse "conversational" / "narration";
          e.g. Rachel, Sarah, Brian, Adam — pick by ear).
- Model:  Eleven Multilingual v2 for a clean natural read, or Eleven v3 for the
          most expressive (v3 also reads CAPS as emphasis and "..." as a pause).
- Stability ~45  ·  Similarity ~75  ·  Style ~0–25  ·  Speaker boost on  ·  Speed 1.0
  (lower Stability = more expressive/varied; nudge up if it gets uneven.)

Delivery intent per line (for a voice actor or v3 tags): warm open landing on
"data"; a touch wry on "messy"; light lift on "say"; emphasize "every row";
emphasize "any"; confident, unhurried close.

Send back the exported WAV (any sample rate). Drop it at out/voiceover-en.wav and
`node audio.mjs` muxes it into both ratios; if the timing drifts, the video beats
get nudged to match the voice.
