# TamedTable demo voiceover: English, 20 seconds.
# The spoken lines are short so each fits its beat at natural pace (no speed-up).
# The on-screen captions carry the fuller wording; the voiceover is punchier.
#
# gemini-tts.mjs synthesizes these with Gemini TTS: it renders the whole
# script in one continuous take, splits the read at its longest pauses, and
# lays the phrases onto the beats (see demo-video-plan.md). For an external
# voice (Azure/Google/Polly/ElevenLabs), export one 20s track to
# out/voiceover-en.wav and mux it over the silent capture instead.
#
# start → spoken line                                  (delivery note)

6.5s   Just say what you want.                         (light lift on "say")

16.0s  Keep the steps, or export to Python.            (confident close)
